# 🚨 ResQAI — Autonomous Disaster Triage & Multi-Agency Intelligence Hub

> An AI-powered disaster intelligence and emergency response coordination platform that transforms fragmented incident signals into actionable, prioritized response intelligence.


👉 **[Launch ResQAI](https://resqai-disaster-response.netlify.app/)**

---

## 📌 Overview

During disasters, critical information comes from multiple fragmented sources such as official reports, SMS messages, social media, phone/radio communications, and citizen reports.

**ResQAI** brings these signals together into a unified intelligence dashboard.

The platform helps emergency response teams:

- Detect and organize incoming disaster signals
- Identify duplicate reports using AI
- Cluster related incidents
- Prioritize critical emergencies
- Identify trapped people and people at risk
- Analyze raw multi-source signals
- Coordinate emergency resources
- Visualize disaster intelligence through an interactive dashboard

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During large-scale emergencies, response teams often receive information from many different channels.

This creates problems such as:

- Duplicate incident reports
- Information overload
- Delayed identification of critical incidents
- Difficulty locating people at risk
- Fragmented information across multiple sources
- Difficulty prioritizing emergency response
- Lack of a unified real-time operational view
